Privacy policy

Apex Recorder · last updated 10 November 2026

Data recorded

Only while a recording is running: sensor motion data, the phone's motion sensors and GPS location, and the rider name chosen in the app.

Use and sharing

Recordings are uploaded to a private Google Drive and used only for analysis. They are not sold or shared.

Google Drive access

The app uses the drive.file scope, so it can only access files it created. Its use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

To have your recordings deleted, email centaur1477@gmail.com.
